⚠️ Missables Watchlist & Caution Protocols

Prevent missed trophies or unnecessary playthrough restarts with these crucial gameplay tips and cautionary checkpoints.

HIGH PRIORITY

Actions Speak Louder (Trophy #48)

Requirement: Make 500 dynamic choices throughout the game.

Caution: GTA VI features dynamic contextual prompts during conversations with your partner, pedestrians, and dynamic world encounters. Rushing purely from mission objective to mission objective without talking to NPCs can leave you short of the 500 choices milestone by the end credits.

Prevention: Always respond to in-car dialogue prompts with Lucia or Jason. Greet or antagonize pedestrians when walking down sidewalks. Never skip optional stranger interactions.

TACTICAL ALERT

Sneakthief (Trophy #39)

Requirement: Rob a jewelry store without being detected.

Caution: If an alarm trips or a bystander flags police before you exit the premises, the stealth requirement is voided. While open-world jewelry stores exist, story opportunities offer scripted ventilation and alarm cutoffs.

Prevention: Save before attempting a jewelry robbery. Cut the external alarm wire box at the rear alley, use sleeping gas through rooftop vents or silenced weapons on security, loot the display cabinets, and leave cleanly.

PARTNER COORDINATION

Retail Therapy (Trophy #34)

Requirement: Rob 3 shops back-to-back with your partner without losing your Wanted Level.

Caution: If your Wanted Level drops to 0 between store 1, 2, or 3, or if your partner is incapacitated or separated, the streak counter resets.

Prevention: Pick 3 adjacent stores along Ocean Boulevard or Downtown Vice City. Keep police cruisers in visual proximity to maintain at least 1-2 stars while driving between the three stores.

PARTNER ESCAPE

Hot Together (Trophy #33)

Requirement: Lose a 4-Star Wanted Level with your partner and escape the heat.

Caution: Both protagonists must survive together in the getaway vehicle without getting arrested or killed.

Prevention: Build up 4 stars using an armored 4-door vehicle, then immediately take the subway tunnel or deep swamp thicket to cut line of sight and hold position until stars clear.

SAFETY PROTOCOL

Manual Save Slots (Chapters 2, 3, 4)

Requirement: Ensure all Strangers and Freaks strands remain completed.

Strategy: Keep 3 rotating manual save slots at the start of Chapter 2, Chapter 3, and Chapter 4 before initiating key campaign heists. If any stranger contact fails or bugs out, you can reload without restarting a 50-hour campaign.
