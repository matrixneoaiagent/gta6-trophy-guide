The Leonida Platinum Walkthrough Strategy

Grand Theft Auto VI features 55 PlayStation 5 trophies (1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze). Obtaining the Platinum trophy "Vice Got a Price!" requires a balanced, sequential approach to prevent missing key story-locked partner actions before endgame branching.

STAGE 1

Main Story Campaign: Lucia & Jason

12 Story Trophies

Immerse yourself into the narrative campaign from Gone Fishing (Prologue) through Chapters 1, 2, 3, 4, and the grand finale You Gotta Know When to Get Off. Enjoy the story naturally while upgrading weapon capacities and practicing dual-character synchronization.

Key Objective: Do not rush past free-roam opportunities in Chapters 2 and 3 when both characters are available for partnership mechanics.
STAGE 2

Missables & Dual-Partner Free Roam Actions

4 Missable Trophies

Before advancing past major story crossroads, knock out all 4 missable trophies that demand specific partner participation and narrative decisions:

  • Retail Therapy: Rob 3 stores consecutively with your partner without losing your wanted level.
  • Hot Together: Evade a 4-star wanted level alongside your partner in free-roam.
  • Sneakthief: Infiltrate and rob a jewelry boutique completely undetected without setting off alarms.
  • Actions Speak Louder: Actively make 500 choices across dialogue and moral branching opportunities.
STAGE 3

Strangers & Freaks Side Strands

8 Trophies

Complete all 8 full Strangers & Freaks storylines across Leonida (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret's legendary beast hunt, and more). These unlock valuable safehouses, airfields, contacts, and contribute directly toward silver trophy Strangers & Freaks.

STAGE 4

100% Activities, Collectibles & Cleanup

31 Trophies

Complete the remaining open-world criteria for Gold trophy A Long Road: Achieve Gold in all 42 Shooting Range challenges, win 14 Top Fun Drone races, conquer 20 street races, discover 100 vehicles, tag 100 WAiNK spots, collect 3 card decks, meditate at 18 spots, and launder $250,000 to unlock Platinum!
