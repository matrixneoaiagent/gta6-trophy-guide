Grand Theft Auto VI offers an expansive dual-protagonist journey following Lucia and Jason across Vice City and the wider State of Leonida. No difficulty-specific trophies exist, meaning you can play on standard settings throughout. Follow our 4-stage sequential walkthrough below to conquer the story, safeguard all missables, wrap up strangers, and polish off 100% completion with zero backtracking headaches.

STAGE 1

Main Story Campaign & Fundamentals

Approx. 35 - 40 Hours • Story Missions & Natural Milestones

Play through the full campaign covering Gone Fishing, Chapters 1 through 4, and the final mission. Familiarize yourself with dual-character controls, weapon wheel management, and Combat Focus abilities. Enjoy the narrative freely while keeping an eye out for conversational choice prompts and ambient robberies.

💡
Pro Strategy: Always maintain separate manual save slots at the start of each new Chapter (especially entering Chapter 2 and Chapter 4). This guarantees a safety net if a missable task is bypassed.
STAGE 2

Missables & High-Heat Partner Trophies

Approx. 6 - 8 Hours • Urgent Priority in Chapter 2 & 3

Before concluding Chapter 4, knock out the 4 missable trophies that require dual-character coordination or choice telemetry:

  • Retail Therapy: Rob 3 shops back-to-back with your partner without losing your Wanted Level.
  • Sneakthief: Rob a jewelry store with zero detection (cut alarms, silent glass cutting).
  • Hot Together: Evade a 4-star Wanted Level while riding with your partner.
  • Actions Speak Louder: Accumulate 500 decision interactions throughout story and ambient encounters.
STAGE 3

Strangers & Freaks, Gang Rackets & Mini-games

Approx. 15 - 20 Hours • Side Quests & Leonida Outlaws

Complete the 8 eccentric Strangers & Freaks storylines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret, and others). Participate in all 9 recreation activities (bowling, fishing vs Cal, arcade machine completion, racing circuit wins), and raid illegal operations belonging to all 9 rival Leonida gangs.

STAGE 4

100% Completion & Collectibles Cleanup

Approx. 20 - 25 Hours • Post-Game Free Roam & Platinum Pop

Take flight in a helicopter to defog all 52 zones of Leonida. Complete all 250 stunts, achieve Gold in all 42 shooting range drills, win all 14 Top Fun drone races, meditate at all 18 zen spots, photograph 100 WAiNK landmarks, complete 3 card sets (60 cards), and purchase all 8 garages. Once the 100% checklist notification appears, the coveted Platinum Vice Got a Price! unlocks!
